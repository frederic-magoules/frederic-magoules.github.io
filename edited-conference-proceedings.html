The Seventh International Conference on Parallel, Distributed, GPU and Cloud Computing for Engineering was held in Pécs, Hungary, from 28 to 31 August 2023, in collaboration with the Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology, University of Pécs. This online volume of Civil-Comp Conference Proceedings contains 10 papers presented at the conference.
