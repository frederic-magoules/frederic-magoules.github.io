Research Themes

This research focuses on numerical analysis, scalable numerical methods and scientific machine learning for scientific and engineering simulation. It addresses partial differential equations (PDEs), numerical algorithms and parallel computation, with particular attention to accuracy, convergence and computational efficiency.

Computational acoustics, mechanics and building energy analysis provide complementary application settings. The three themes connect mathematical properties, computational performance and validation against physical models or data. Domain decomposition forms one of the main methodological pillars of this research.

1. Numerical Analysis and Discretization of PDEs 2. Scalable Numerical Methods and High-Performance Computing 3. Scientific Machine Learning and Hybrid Models

Theme 1

Numerical Analysis and Discretization of PDEs

Scientific problem

PDE simulations depend on discretizations, boundary operators and linear solvers whose accuracy and convergence must be understood for oscillatory problems, noncoercive operators and ill-conditioned linear systems.

Contributions

Contributions to the mathematical analysis of numerical methods for PDEs address acoustic-wave dispersion, boundary absorption and parametric optimization. They also include superlinear Krylov convergence for complex noncoercive operators, the spectral properties of delayed gradient methods, and algebraic approximations of Dirichlet-to-Neumann maps in linear elasticity.

Methods

  • Finite element formulations and three-dimensional dispersion analysis for acoustics.
  • Boundary absorption and non-Lipschitz parametric optimization.
  • Operator-based preconditioning, Krylov convergence and spectral analysis of gradient methods.
  • Algebraic approximation of Dirichlet-to-Neumann maps for linear elasticity.

Applications

Computational acoustics, wave propagation, noise absorption and linear elasticity.

Theme 2

Scalable Numerical Methods and High-Performance Computing

Scientific problem

Large PDE systems require algorithms that use parallel resources efficiently. Communication, synchronization and uneven workloads can limit performance, while convergence and stopping criteria must remain dependable.

Contributions

Contributions to scalable iterative methods for PDEs and large linear systems include optimized Schwarz algorithms, discrete transmission conditions and coarse-space corrections. They also cover asynchronous approaches to global–local coupling and time integration, together with the analysis of how their convergence properties translate into parallel implementations.

Methods

  • Optimized Schwarz methods, discrete transmission conditions and non-invasive global–local coupling.
  • Coarse-space correction, algebraic interface operators and preconditioning.
  • Asynchronous iterations and Parareal time integration.
  • Distributed-memory parallel implementation and convergence monitoring.

Applications

Parallel PDE simulation, computational acoustics, structural mechanics, plasticity and large linear systems.

Theme 3

Scientific Machine Learning and Hybrid Models

Scientific problem

Learning methods can support PDE simulation and prediction from data. The challenge is to choose models that make effective use of physical information, numerical representations and available observations, and to assess their accuracy on the intended application.

Contributions

Research in scientific machine learning addresses PDE simulation and prediction from data. Contributions include physics-informed graph-mesh networks, hybrid combinations of numerical and learned models, and methodological reviews of learning approaches. Support vector machines and neural networks are also applied to building energy prediction, fault detection and diagnosis.

Methods

  • Physics-informed learning and graph representations of PDE meshes.
  • Hybrid combinations of numerical solvers and learned models.
  • Support vector machines, neural networks and data mining.
  • Evaluation of prediction accuracy and fault detection performance.

Applications

PDE simulation on complex meshes, building energy prediction, fault detection and diagnosis, and industrial numerical simulation.

Academic and industrial collaboration

Across these themes, mathematical analysis guides the choice of algorithms, parallel implementation determines their practical efficiency, and comparisons with physical models or observations support validation. Related application examples are shown in the Scientific Gallery.
